Kitchen Desk

Privacy

This policy covers the Kitchen Desk website and the related personal automation that posts to Owen Sheehan’s own Pinterest Business boards.

Operator

Owen Sheehan · owen.sheehan@vaaltd.co.uk

What data is used

  • Pinterest account credentials and tokens you authorise for the Kitchen Desk automation
  • Amazon Associates / Product Advertising credentials used to look up products and build affiliate links
  • Product metadata (title, image, price, ASIN) used only to create Pins on your own boards

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not offer this app to other users
  • We do not collect Pinterest user data beyond the operator’s own account

Storage

Credentials for the automation are stored locally on the operator’s machine in private, non-public configuration files. This website itself does not run a user login or collect form submissions.
